# KIRO JUMP (My first Game as a project)

A game done through my free time to entertain myself and to train myself on game development.

Game development is a hobby for me. to pass time and to escape from our horrible reality.

## Game Story

The game is starting with

[](/karmontal/Kiro-Jump/blob/master/assets/kiro/kiro.apng) **KIRO** a white simple alien who needs to escape this so called _**LATA**_ planet by jumping space jumps in the two directions and to avoid the obstacles in the way. The thing is that he doesn't know how much he needs to jump into space. Because _**GRAVITT**_ Kiros' enemy is too powerful with his gravity force that pulls Kiro down and also _**OBSY**_ the obstacles maker who tries to close Kiros' way into space.

GRAVITT: NO ESCAPE FROM MY PLANET !!!

KIRO: I need to get back home.

GRAVITT: BUT I NEED YOU TO STAY WITH ME.

GRAVITT: OBSYYYY !!!

OBSY: Yes Griv?

GRAVITT: HELP ME AND STOP HIM !!!

OBSY: Will do my friend !

Will you help **KIRO** to escape _**LATA**_ Planet.

## MANUS AI Breif

Based on your description, here's a summary of the core concept:

**Game Name** : Kiro Jump

**Genre** : Pixel Art Endless Runner with Roguelite elements.

**Story** : Kiro, a simple alien, is trapped on Planet Lata and must ascend by jumping to escape.

**Gameplay** : Players control Kiro, jumping upwards to achieve the highest score possible while avoiding obstacles.

**Enemies** :

  * _**Gravitt**_ : Creates gravity pulls to drag Kiro down.
  * _**Obsy**_ : Places physical obstacles in Kiro's path.



**Engine** : Godot (for multi-platform support).

This is a solid foundation. To build the "best version" of this game, we need to flesh out the details, especially the roguelite elements and the core gameplay loop.

Here are the next things we should decide on:

  * **Roguelite Upgrades** : What kind of temporary, run-based upgrades can Kiro get? These are key to making every playthrough feel different. For example:
  * **Jump Enhancements** : Higher Jump, Double Jump, a Ground-Slam attack?
  * **Enemy Counters** : A temporary shield against obstacles, a way to reverse Gravitt's pull, or a short-range dash to phase through things?
  * **Scoring Bonuses** : A score multiplier, or a magnet to attract collectibles?
  * **Obstacles & Environment**: Besides the enemies, what does Planet Lata look like? What kind of obstacles will Obsy create? Are they static platforms, moving platforms, crumbling blocks, or laser beams? Does the environment change the higher Kiro gets (e.g., from a rocky surface to a stormy atmosphere)?



**Controls** : How will the player control Kiro?

  * _**Mobile**_ : Tilting the phone to move left/right and tapping to use an ability?
  * _**PC/Web**_ : Using arrow keys or "A" and "D" to move, and the spacebar for abilities?



**Permanent Progression** : What can players unlock permanently between runs to feel a sense of progress? Maybe a currency collected during runs can be used to unlock new starting abilities, different Kiro "skins," or new enemy types to encounter?
